IMPORT SETTINGS
Choose millimetres. STL itself does not record units.
Source scene metres converted to millimetres at 1:20 scale (50 mm per scene unit).
Z is up. Each model is centered in X/Y and its lowest vertex is at Z=0.
For full-size scene geometry, scale the import by 20 (2000%).

These are binary STL snapshots of the simulator geometry, not CAD solids.
STL does not preserve colors, glass transparency, materials, labels or animation.
Moving flow markers, lights, cameras and interface elements are excluded.

3D PRINTING
These visual meshes have not been prepared or validated for fabrication.
They contain separate/overlapping objects, fine details and open-ended tubes.
Glass shelves become opaque surfaces. The inspection circuit is detached.
Before printing, repair/union geometry as appropriate, close openings, thicken
small features, arrange disconnected parts, and inspect supports in your slicer.
The complete kitchen exceeds many desktop printer beds at the supplied scale.
